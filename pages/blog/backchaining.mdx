---
title: "Thinking tool: Backwards chaining"
publishedOn: "2026-07-29"
---

When trying to brainstorm options to achieve a goal: identify that goal and work backwards from it.

This is as opposed to forward chaining, where you consider your current position (for example by performing a [SWOT analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SWOT_analysis)) and brainstorm possible things you could do from there.

Backchaining often seems to generate more ambitious or on-target ideas than forward chaining. It's not always better, for example forward chaining can be hugely valuable for [creating surface area for luck](../create-surface-area-for-luck/). But generally nudging yourself towards backchaining is helpful given often people's "default" mode of brainstorming is forward chaining.
