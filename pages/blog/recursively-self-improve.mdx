> Give a man a fish and he'll eat for a day. Teach a man to fish and he'll eat for a lifetime.
>
> Teach a man to teach himself, and umm... he'll improve all wellbeing outcomes for the beings in his moral circle?

Investing in improving yourself is usually a smart move. What's even more powerful is when this compounds - i.e. when improving yourself also makes you better at improving your future self.

![Two curves of capability over time from the same start: 'standard 1% growth' is a straight line; 'recursive 1% growth' curves upward, overtaking it and accelerating away](../../images/blog/thinking-tools-to-be-more-effective-at-anything/recursive-growth.svg)

It's therefore worth thinking about how you can improve yourself in this way. I think practising improving your general thinking skills helps here. Additionally, favouring improvements to the improvement process itself through meta-retros and post-mortems, or feedback loops on your [feedback loops](../build-feedback-loops/).
